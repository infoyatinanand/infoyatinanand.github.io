Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-08

Personal Job Tracker is used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account.

What it accesses

  • Google Sheets and Drive: to read and write the owner's own job-tracking spreadsheet.
  • Gmail: to read job-related emails in the owner's own mailbox.

How data is used

Data is used only to run the tool for its owner. Job-related text may be sent to AI service providers (such as Anthropic and Google Gemini) to analyse it. Data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Where data is stored

In the owner's own Google Sheet, on the owner's own computer, and in the owner's own private storage. Access tokens are kept as private secrets and are never published.

Google API data

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account permissions page, and delete the spreadsheet and local files.
